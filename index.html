About

At Tata Digital I was a backend engineer in the flights and hotels team of the Tata Neu app. Together with one teammate I designed and built the Pax Service, which gives the travel systems scoped access to verified passenger data without ever exposing the customer master record behind it. I also moved our event sync from Azure EventHub to Kafka without downtime, and wrote the reconciliation logic that brought identity mismatches down from about 15 a week to one or two.

For my last six months I joined a newly formed product catalogue team under the VP of Technology. I worked on Java (Spring Boot) services there and also took over the team's cloud setup and CI/CD.

A lot of my day-to-day work was about keeping customer data correct and in sync, and I wanted to understand the statistics and machine learning behind data work properly. That's why I came to Dortmund for my Master's. Since then I've worked on simulation-based inference for gravitational lenses and built a small fraud detection pipeline on Kafka.
