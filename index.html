01 · About

From production systems to data science

At Tata Digital I worked on the travel (flights and hotels) category of the Tata Neu super-app. With one teammate I designed and built the Pax Service, a privacy-first layer over Tata's Golden Record that gives the travel systems only the customer data they need. I moved our event sync from Azure EventHub to Apache Kafka with zero downtime, and wrote reconciliation logic that cut identity mismatches from about 15 a week to 1 or 2.

In my last six months I joined a brand-new team that reported to the VP of Technology. I took over infrastructure and CI/CD, which were normally handled by separate central teams. That cut new service setup from 7 to 10 days down to 1 to 2.

Working with data every day made me want to go deeper into the statistics and machine learning side, so I moved to Germany for my Master's. I've since built a simulation-based inference pipeline for gravitational lenses and a real-time fraud detection pipeline on Kafka.
